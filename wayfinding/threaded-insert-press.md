# Threaded insert press

### _<mark style="color:orange;">We are still working to adding information on this tool. In the mean time, look for a Creative Technology Assistant or reach out to cats@brown.edu if you have questions.</mark>_

The heated insert press is designed to help with the installation of threaded inserts into 3D printed parts. Threaded inserts can make 3D printed parts stronger and can last longer. For small screws they can also be easier to use than trying to design fine thread into 3D prints.

The press uses a soldering iron fitted with a special tip designed to fit the inserts.

### Designing for threaded inserts

Before using the press you must design a hole for the threaded insert in your 3D print. Each insert is different but it should be just begin enough for the edge of the insert to fit. The texture part of the insert should not fit into the hole as the goal is to melt the plastic around it to get a tight fit.

### Getting Started:&#x20;

1. Turn the soldering iron on to approximately 210° C This is the approximate melting temperture of PLA.
2. Place the part below the iron and set the insert into the 3D printed part.&#x20;
3. Press gently but firmly until the insert sits flush with the surface of the 3D printed part.
4. Turn off the soldering iron when you are done.
