Key features (from the video)

* AI noise reduction that learns from your environment and removes hums, buzzes, and street noise while keeping voice natural.
* Tone control (EQ and dynamics) for warmth and refinement.
* Portable design with flexible power options: up to \~3.5 hours on AA batteries or unlimited when powered via USB (power bank / laptop).
* Four XLR inputs with up to +70 dB gain and 48V phantom power on every channel.
* Remote guest inputs via USB, Bluetooth, or smartphone (bring in up to two guests while using remaining inputs).
* Built-in mix-minus to remove echo and feedback.
* Four headphone outputs, each with independent volume control.
* Backlit controls, mute buttons, dedicated volume knobs, and a bright display for quick monitoring.
* Records 48 kHz / 24-bit audio (suitable for video production).
* Four sound pads for music, effects, intros — record to pad or load custom sounds.
* Save a stereo publish-ready mix and individual channel multitracks to microSD (up to 2 TB).
* Works as a plug-and-play USB audio interface for Mac, PC, iOS, Android with full multitrack compatibility.

The key to any great podcast is great sound. And with the Zoom PodTrak P4 Next, you get professional level audio at your fingertips. AI noise reduction learns from your environment and removes hums, buzzes, and even street noise while keeping your voice sounding clear and natural. I have it turned on right now so you can hear the difference. Then there is tone control, giving your voice warmth and refinement through EQ and DSing, almost like having a studio engineer at your side. The P4X isn't just powerful, it's portable. Its compact design and versatile power delivery options let you record anywhere. Run up to 3 and 1/2 hours on AA batteries, or connect via USB to your laptop or power bank and keep rolling for as long as you need.

With four XLR inputs providing up to plus 70 dB of gain and 48vt phantom power on every channel, you can even use the most demanding broadcast mics with ease. Another cool feature is that if you have remote guests on your podcast, you can bring in up to two guests through USB, Bluetooth, or smartphone input while using the remaining inputs for a five-person show. The built-in mix minus removes echo and feedback automatically. And with four headphone outputs, each with its own volume control, everyone hears exactly what they need. No more sharing adapters or splitters. Everything on the P4X is designed for speed and simplicity. Back lit controls put record and playback at your fingertips. Mute buttons keep things clean when you're off mic. Dedicated volume knobs make mixing easy. Plus, the bright display shows track levels and settings at a glance, so you can focus on your conversation and not your gear.

For video creators, the P4X records in 48 kHz 24-bit audio, the standard for video production. Plug it into your phone directly to capture pro-level audio directly to your video clips. No post-production required. Or use it as an interface for live streams that sound every bit as professional as they look. Connected via USBC, the P4X works as a plug-and-play audio interface for Mac, PC, iOS, or Android with full multitrack compatibility.
