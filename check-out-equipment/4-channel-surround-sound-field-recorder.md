# 4 Channel Surround Sound Field Recorder

[https://zoomcorp.com/en/us/handheld-recorders/handheld-recorders/h2n-handy-recorder/](https://zoomcorp.com/en/us/handheld-recorders/handheld-recorders/h2n-handy-recorder/)\


This is a versatile audio recorder with 5 built in microphones and most notably 4 different recording modes, allowing you to record spatial or surround sound audio.&#x20;

You may also use this as a high-quality usb microphone for recording onto your computer or for a live-streaming call.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/5Mics_1.jpg.900x1000_q70_crop_focal_area-1551,1818,1759,2688_size_canvas.jpg" alt=""><figcaption><p>Calling Foley Artists</p></figcaption></figure>

You may plug an additional lavalier microphone into the Zoom H2n using phantom power.&#x20;

**WHY USE THIS OVER THE** [**H1n**](basic-recorder-kit.md) **OR** [**M4**](audio-kit/)**???**

Mainly, this field recorder does _**not**_ have an XLR inputs like the M4, so you would be sacrificing quality in that respect if you were to use an even higher quality (or directional) microphone such as is provided in the [Shotgun Mic Kit](shotgun-mic-kit/).&#x20;

However should you be only using the onboard microphone on the M4 or the H2n then the portability and discrete size of the H2n is advantageous. The H2n is nearly a quarter of the M4's size. It also works well in capturing ambient sound. This can also be great for recording live music/rehearsals, at least on a more casual basis.&#x20;

The mic's surround sound polar patterns (the shape and direction where it records) are primarily built for capturing ambient sound, so it will not isolate multiple voices speaking independently unless the room is very sound-neutral or silent.&#x20;

&#x20;If you're not concerned about having a USB microphone or with capturing ambient sound at the highest quality possible, you may prefer the small no-nonsense design of the H1n. &#x20;

To review:&#x20;

**For more serious film or video - Favor the M4 with an XLR microphone or the** [**Field Recorders.** ](field-recorder.md)

<mark style="background-color:red;">**For more casual film and on-the go as well as ambient sound capture - Favor the H2n**</mark>

**For more casual (voice notes, basic interviews, discrete recordings) - Favor the H1n**
